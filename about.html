Milly John’s Burgers is a fast-food restaurant that was established in 2024 to provide delicious and affordable meals to ordinary South Africans. The restaurant was created with the goal of serving fresh, tasty burgers and quality meals without compromising affordability or customer satisfaction.

Our mission is to serve flavourful burgers made from the freshest ingredients while ensuring that every customer leaves with a warm smile and an enjoyable experience.

Our vision is to become the most trusted and loved burger restaurant in South Africa and eventually expand globally, connecting people through quality food and memorable dining experiences.

Milly John’s Burgers mainly targets ordinary South Africans by offering meals starting from as little as R30. However, the restaurant welcomes everyone because inclusivity, affordability and quality service are at the heart of the business.

Despite offering affordable prices, Milly John’s Burgers remains committed to providing top-tier quality meals, excellent customer service and a welcoming environment for all customers.
